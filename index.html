Your website stays yours.

ReIndexMe is being designed around a simple trust model: inspect only what the user explicitly asks to inspect, without turning the product into another webmaster account.

No accountNo ReIndexMe login is required to use the core tools.
No domain verificationYou do not need to prove ownership of a site to ReIndexMe.
No permanent site accessThe Local Inspector will analyze the active page only when you trigger it.
No permanent URL collectionThe product is not built around storing a history of pages you inspect.

Local Inspector is coming next.

The browser extension will inspect the page you are currently viewing and report indexability, canonical signals, metadata, structured data and other discovery clues. Chrome and Edge links will appear here when the extensions are published.
