04 · Local Inspector

The browser extension is designed around user-triggered local inspection.

ReIndexMe Local Inspector is intended to analyze the page the user explicitly chooses to inspect, helping identify signals such as indexability, canonical configuration, metadata, structured data and related discovery information.

The extension is designed around the principles of no ReIndexMe account, no ReIndexMe domain verification, no permanent site access and no permanent URL collection as part of the core inspection workflow.
