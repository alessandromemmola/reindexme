Your website stays yours.

ReIndexMe uses a simple trust model: inspect only what you explicitly choose to inspect, without turning the product into another webmaster account.

No accountNo ReIndexMe login is required to use the core tools.
No domain verificationYou do not need to prove ownership of a site to ReIndexMe.
No permanent site accessLocal Inspector analyzes the active page only when you trigger it.
No permanent URL collectionThe product is not built around storing a history of pages you inspect.

Local Inspector is available now.

ReIndexMe Local Inspector v0.1.0 checks the active page on demand for titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, robots directives, structured data, social metadata and other on-page signals. The report stays in your browser; you can copy it when you choose. Available now for Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

Get for Chrome → Get for Microsoft Edge →

For website owners: optional external tools such as PageSpeed Insights and Rich Results Test provide separate technical checks. They do not force crawling or indexing.
